Services

Each service does one task and does not call another. A trigger can only use a service in the countries where it is enabled and while its status is Active.

Positions & terms

The position catalog is global. Each country has its own search terms for those positions: a position is searched in a country when it has terms there. Each term belongs to one position and one country, and enters inactive in every trigger.

Geography

Country, division and city. Each country uses one division level, identified by its ISO 3166-2 code. Each service names a city in its own way (alias). A city a service returns that is not in the catalog is kept in the vacancy and waits here for review.

Decision-maker roles

The roles the decision-maker analysis assigns, the titles they are searched with, and what the engine does with each one.

The level comes from the role, not from the company size. General-management roles apply to every area; the others only to companies whose openings are in their areas.

Prompts by country

Each country has one prompt for each analysis, with its model, text and version. The company prompt carries the ICP of the country and its minimum score.

A country needs its three active prompts to be used in a trigger. A new version is a draft until it is activated; activating it keeps the previous one to go back to.

Company rules

The rules the company services and analysis apply: which companies are excluded, which data a company needs to be qualified, and how long a result stays valid.

Scheduled sends

Cold outreach is not a trigger. Each scheduled send builds a lead list with its filter on its days and hands it to one cold outreach service.

A service only receives leads already filtered. It does not choose them: the filter of the send decides which decision makers enter the list.

Showcase

The talent the booking landing shows below the calendar. Each role carries its own résumé; only roles in the showcase appear, up to the limit.
